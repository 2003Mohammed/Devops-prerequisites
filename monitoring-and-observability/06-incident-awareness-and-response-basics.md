# 06 – Incident Awareness and Response Basics

## Purpose
Monitoring and alerting are only useful if teams know **how to respond**.
This file introduces the role of observability in incident response.

---

## What Is an Incident?

An incident is any event that:
- Degrades service quality
- Disrupts users
- Risks system stability

Not all incidents are outages, but all require attention.

---

## Role of Observability During Incidents

Observability helps teams:
- Identify affected components
- Understand system behavior
- Reduce time to resolution

Clear data leads to faster decisions.

---

## Real DevOps Scenario

Alert triggers for high latency  
Metrics show resource pressure  
Logs identify recent configuration change  
Action taken quickly to restore service

---

## Key Takeaway
Effective incident response depends on **clear signals, not assumptions**.
