# 04 – Alerting Basics and Why It Matters

## Purpose
Alerting ensures that **failures are noticed immediately**, not after users complain.
In DevOps, alerting is the bridge between monitoring data and human action.

---

## What Alerting Does

Alerting helps teams:
- Detect failures in real time
- Respond quickly to incidents
- Reduce downtime

Monitoring without alerting delays response.

---

## When Alerts Are Triggered

Alerts are typically based on:
- Resource thresholds (CPU, memory, disk)
- Service availability
- Error rates
- Latency increases

Alerts should signal **actionable problems**, not noise.

---

## Real DevOps Scenario

Issue: Disk fills up overnight  
Without alerts: Service fails in the morning  
With alerts: Cleanup action taken before failure

---

## Key Takeaway
Good alerting enables fast response and prevents small issues from becoming outages.
