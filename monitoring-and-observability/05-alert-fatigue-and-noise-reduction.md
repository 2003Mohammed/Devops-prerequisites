# 05 – Alert Fatigue and Noise Reduction

## Purpose
Too many alerts reduce effectiveness.
This file explains why **alert quality matters more than alert quantity**.

---

## What Is Alert Fatigue?

Alert fatigue occurs when:
- Alerts fire too frequently
- Alerts trigger without real impact
- Engineers start ignoring notifications

This leads to missed critical incidents.

---

## Why Noise Is Dangerous

Excessive alerts cause:
- Slower response times
- Burnout
- Loss of trust in monitoring systems

Silenced alerts are often worse than no alerts.

---

## Real DevOps Scenario

System sends alerts every minute for minor spikes  
Critical alert occurs and is missed  
Result: Prolonged outage

---

## Key Takeaway
Alerts must be **meaningful, actionable, and minimal** to remain effective.
